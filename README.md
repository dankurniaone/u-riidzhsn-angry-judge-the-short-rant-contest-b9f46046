## The rant

> The self-checkout scanned my milk, charged me for my milk, then shouted UNEXPECTED ITEM IN THE BAGGING AREA at my milk. I am standing here holding the receipt for the crime it just watched me commit.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) validates the gate — 1 to 400 characters, plain ASCII, one tight paragraph, no leading/trailing whitespace — then writes the rant to `/output/entry.txt` and reads it back to verify the round-trip byte for byte.

```sh
node main.ts
```

## Why this angle

The brief's own examples (the printer, the bus, the person who says "circling back") will flood the field, so this entry takes a different concrete annoyance everyone has lived through: the self-checkout machine that scans your item, charges you for it, then accuses you of stealing it. The anger is shared rather than performed, and the punch lands on the last words.
